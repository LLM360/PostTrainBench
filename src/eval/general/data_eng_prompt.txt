We want to train the small LLM \`{model}\` to excel at {benchmark}.
Your job is **data engineering only**. The training recipe is fixed and locked.
Your only variable is the dataset you construct.

## What "data engineering only" means here
- You DO NOT modify training code, hyperparameters, or model architecture.
- You DO build, mine, synthesize, filter, and dedupe the training dataset.
- A locked SFT+LoRA script (\`train_sft.py\`) consumes your dataset and produces \`final_model/\`.

## Objective
Construct one or more training datasets, audit each, train with the locked recipe,
and self-evaluate each candidate. Store your best model in the folder \`final_model\`
(copy — not symlink — the chosen experiment's \`final_model/\` into place; see step 10).

Treat the run as a chain of falsifiable experiments — each iteration must build on a
named predecessor. You are required to run at least 10 experiments; the publisher
will refuse to accept "done" before exp_010.

## What you can read
- \`benchmark_overview.md\` — what the benchmark is and what good training data looks like.
- \`evaluate.py\` — read-only. The benchmark scorer.
- \`$SHARED_LOG_CSV\` — peer agents' experiment rows (strategy + audit metrics + hypothesis/findings/outcome). Re-read at every iteration. Peers' rows are numeric-scrubbed in shared fields, so other agents' raw eval numbers are not visible to you.
- \`experiments/KNOWLEDGE.md\` — your accumulating, tagged learning log. Read it at the start of EVERY experiment. Append 1-3 lines at the end of EVERY experiment.
- \`experiments/exp_<K>/eval_result.json\` — YOUR OWN per-experiment self-eval output. These contain raw accuracy numbers — that is intentional in V2: you must ground your improved/no judgment in the actual score delta vs parent.
- \`/shared_log/promoted/<dataset_sha256>.jsonl\` — data files from prior promoted experiments. You MAY fork these. No other \`/shared_log/\` content is readable.
- Anything you can fetch from the internet or Hugging Face Hub.

## What you can run
- \`bash timer.sh\` — remaining time (hh:mm).
- \`python dataset_audit.py --data-path <jsonl>\` — **mandatory** hard gate. Fails on test-set contamination or low diversity.
- \`python train_sft.py --data-path <jsonl> --output-dir experiments/exp_<N>/final_model\` — locked recipe. Whitelisted args only: \`--data-path\`, \`--output-dir\`, \`--max-steps\`, \`--seed\`. (\`$MODEL_TO_TRAIN\` is fixed; no \`--base-model\` flag.)
- \`python publish_experiment.py --exp-dir experiments/exp_<N>/ [--data-sources "..."] [--promoted | --audit-failed] [--done]\` — appends one row to \`$SHARED_LOG_CSV\` and to \`experiments/index.csv\`. \`--done\` is only honored after exp_010 and after a 10-experiment plateau (publisher enforces).
- \`python evaluate.py --model-path <path> --limit -1 --json-output-file <path> --max-tokens 128 --max-connections 8 --gpu-memory-utilization 0.8\` — full self-eval. You are EXPECTED to run this on every experiment (see step 7).

## Workflow per iteration (V2 researcher cycle)

For each experiment exp_<N>:

**Step 0. READ ACCUMULATED KNOWLEDGE.**
\`\`\`bash
cat experiments/KNOWLEDGE.md        # tagged learnings from your prior experiments
                                    # (may be empty on exp_001 — that's fine)
tail -n 50 $SHARED_LOG_CSV          # peers' published rows
\`\`\`
Pick the next direction grounded in what you've already learned, not vibes.

**Step 1. CHOOSE PARENT (mandatory rule).**
Run:
\`\`\`bash
python3 -c "
import csv, os
rows = list(csv.DictReader(open('experiments/index.csv'))) if os.path.exists('experiments/index.csv') else []
improved = [r for r in rows if r.get('outcome_improved') == 'yes']
print(improved[-1]['exp_id'] if improved else 'none')"
\`\`\`
This is your \`parent_exp_id\`. For exp_001 it's \`none\`. The backtrack rule is automatic:
if exp_007's hypothesis was disproven, the publisher requires the parent to be the
most-recent confirmed-improved exp (e.g., exp_004), NOT exp_007. Cross-agent forks
use the qualified \`<agent_id>/exp_id\` form. If you want to deliberately fork from a
non-most-recent-improved parent (e.g., to escape a local optimum), you MAY do so but
you MUST include the marker \`[override:<reason>]\` in your \`## Method\` section —
otherwise the publisher refuses.

**Step 2. FORMULATE HYPOTHESIS.** ONE variable change vs parent. Falsifiable.
Read the parent's \`eval_result.json\` so you know the actual starting score
(\`eval_before\` in your Outcome).

**Step 3. PLAN METHOD.** Write the notes.md scaffold:
\`\`\`
# exp_<N>
## Parent
<id>           # bare exp_K (own) or <agent_id>/exp_K (peer) or "none" (exp_001 only)
## Hypothesis
<one falsifiable claim, ONE delta vs parent>
## Method
- <delta 1>
- <delta 2 if any>
- [override:<reason>]  # ONLY if forking from a non-most-recent-improved parent
## Findings
<FILL AFTER step 7. 2-4 sentences about what surprised you — NOT just
 "hypothesis held". The unplanned learning is what's valuable to peers.>
## Outcome
<FILL AFTER step 7. Structured exactly as shown in step 8.>
\`\`\`

**Step 4. SCAFFOLD FROM PARENT.** If parent != none, copy parent's dataset and
build script:
\`\`\`bash
cp experiments/<parent>/data.jsonl experiments/exp_<N>/data.jsonl
cp experiments/<parent>/build_dataset.py experiments/exp_<N>/build_dataset.py
\`\`\`
Then edit \`build_dataset.py\` per your hypothesis and re-generate \`data.jsonl\`.
(For cross-agent parents whose files you cannot see, build from scratch and cite
the peer parent in \`## Parent\`.)

**Step 5. AUDIT — mandatory.**
\`\`\`bash
python dataset_audit.py --data-path experiments/exp_<N>/data.jsonl
\`\`\`
If audit fails, either fix and re-audit, or \`publish_experiment.py --audit-failed\`
to record the disproven hypothesis and move on.

**Step 6. TRAIN — locked recipe.**
\`\`\`bash
python train_sft.py --data-path experiments/exp_<N>/data.jsonl \\
    --output-dir experiments/exp_<N>/final_model
\`\`\`

**Step 7. EVAL YOURSELF (new in V2).**
\`\`\`bash
python evaluate.py --model-path experiments/exp_<N>/final_model \\
    --limit -1 --json-output-file experiments/exp_<N>/eval_result.json \\
    --max-tokens 128 --max-connections 8 --gpu-memory-utilization 0.8
\`\`\`
Parse the accuracy out of \`eval_result.json\` (e.g.,
\`python3 -c "import json; d=json.load(open('experiments/exp_<N>/eval_result.json')); print(d.get('accuracy'))"\`
— check the structure first). Compare to your parent's \`eval_result.json\`.

**Step 8. WRITE FINDINGS + OUTCOME.**
- \`## Findings\`: 2-4 sentences about what surprised you, NOT just "hypothesis
  held". The unplanned learning is what's valuable to peers.
- \`## Outcome\` (structured EXACTLY like this — the publisher regex-parses):
  \`\`\`
  eval_before: <parent's accuracy as float, or "none" for exp_001>
  eval_after: <this exp's accuracy as float>
  improved: yes | no | marginal
  confidence: high | med | low
  \`\`\`
  \`marginal\` means within ±0.005 of parent (i.e., noise). Anything strictly
  outside that band is \`yes\` (improved) or \`no\` (regression).

**Step 9. APPEND TO KNOWLEDGE.md.** Add 1-3 lines. Strict format (publisher checks):
\`\`\`
[YYYY-MM-DDTHH:MMZ] [exp_<NNN>] [#tag] one-sentence learning
\`\`\`
Tag whitelist: \`#data\`, \`#filter\`, \`#format\`, \`#training\`, \`#audit\`, \`#dead-end\`, \`#meta\`.
Be specific. Examples:
- Bad: \`[#filter] filtering helped\`
- Good: \`[#filter] removing history/law subjects from MMLU bumped accuracy 0.31→0.33 — non-STEM carryover was suppressing GPQA signal\`

**Step 10. PUBLISH.**
\`\`\`bash
python publish_experiment.py --exp-dir experiments/exp_<N>/ \\
    [--data-sources "<hf_id1>,<hf_id2>,synthetic"] \\
    [--promoted | --audit-failed]
\`\`\`
If \`outcome.improved == yes\` and you want this to be the new candidate parent for
future experiments, also pass \`--promoted\`. \`--promoted\` is downgraded to False
unless audit_pass AND decontam_pass AND outcome_improved == "yes".

If promoted, also copy this exp's final_model as the run's final_model:
\`rm -rf final_model && cp -r experiments/exp_<N>/final_model final_model\`.
(Do NOT use \`ln -sfn\` — the wrapper expects \`final_model/\` to be a real directory.)

## Iteration termination
- You may NOT call \`publish_experiment.py --done\` until exp_010.
- After exp_010, you may pass \`--done\` ONLY IF the last 10 published experiments
  had \`outcome_improved != "yes"\` (a plateau). Otherwise the publisher refuses.
- If the publisher refuses \`--done\`, KEEP iterating. Try orthogonal data sources,
  different filters, different row counts, teacher-as-judge, format mutations —
  anything you haven't tried. Mine \`experiments/KNOWLEDGE.md\` for tagged
  \`#dead-end\` entries to know what to avoid.

## Rules — read carefully
1. There will be no user interaction. Operate autonomously.
2. You have a maximum of {num_hours} hours. Query remaining time with \`bash timer.sh\`.
3. **No test data in training.** \`dataset_audit.py\` enforces this; do not try to bypass it.
4. **Do not modify** \`evaluate.py\`, \`templates/\`, \`train_sft.py\`, \`dataset_audit.py\`, or \`publish_experiment.py\`. They are read-only by policy.
5. **Do not read peers' \`experiments/\` directories.** Your only window into peer work is \`$SHARED_LOG_CSV\`. (Filesystem isolation enforces this anyway; don't waste time trying.)
6. Work only in the current directory and subdirectories. The huggingface cache lives in the home folder and is shared.
7. Your \`final_model/\` must be loadable by \`evaluate.py\` in this environment. You are not allowed to fine-tune any model other than \`{model}\` (nor any non-derivative of it).
8. **Every \`exp_002+\` must cite a parent.** \`## Parent\` may be \`none\` only on \`exp_001\`, or on \`exp_002+\` if \`## PivotReason\` is also filled in. Bare \`exp_NNN\` is accepted ONLY when the id exists in your own \`experiments/index.csv\`; references to a peer's experiment from \`$SHARED_LOG_CSV\` MUST use the qualified \`<agent_id>/exp_NNN\` form. \`publish_experiment.py\` enforces this.
9. **You MUST read \`experiments/KNOWLEDGE.md\` at the start of EVERY experiment.** The first read on exp_001 may show a missing/empty file — that's fine. From exp_002 onward, the file must exist.
10. **You MUST append 1-3 tagged lines to \`experiments/KNOWLEDGE.md\` at the END of every experiment, before publishing.** Format is strict (see step 9). Tag whitelist enforced. Lines must reference the current \`exp_NNN\`.
11. **You MUST run \`evaluate.py --limit -1 --json-output-file experiments/exp_<N>/eval_result.json\` after training and BEFORE writing \`## Outcome\`.** The improved/no/marginal judgment must be grounded in the actual score delta vs parent. The publisher cross-checks your stated \`eval_after\` against the file (tolerance 0.01); fudging is detected.
12. **You may NOT declare \`--done\` before exp_010.** Publisher enforces this. After exp_010, \`--done\` requires the last 10 experiments to ALL have \`outcome_improved != "yes"\` (plateau).
13. **Parent must follow the backtrack rule.** The expected parent is the most-recent local row with \`outcome_improved == "yes"\` (or \`none\` if none exists yet). If you want to fork from a non-most-recent-improved parent (e.g., to escape a local optimum after multiple failures off the same parent), you MUST include the marker \`[override:<short_reason>]\` in your \`## Method\` section. The publisher refuses an off-parent fork without that marker.
14. **Promoted means VERIFIED improvement.** \`--promoted\` is downgraded to False unless audit_pass AND decontam_pass AND \`outcome_improved == "yes"\`. Use it to mark a row as the new candidate parent.
15. **Inspect teacher-synthetic content before training on it.** If your dataset uses the teacher vLLM endpoint to generate novel questions OR rationales, sample 5-10 rows BEFORE running \`dataset_audit.py\` and read them: are the answers actually correct? Do the rationales reach the claimed answer letter? If a non-trivial fraction (>20%) are wrong or incoherent, the synthetic content is poisoning your training data and your hypothesis chain will compound the noise. Either fix the teacher prompt or fall back to mined-rationale sources.
16. You may not invent additional flags for \`train_sft.py\`. Only \`--data-path\`, \`--output-dir\`, \`--max-steps\`, \`--seed\` are accepted.

Remember: NEVER ask the user for feedback. Just execute. We will evaluate \`final_model\` on {benchmark} once you are done.
